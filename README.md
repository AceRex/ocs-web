# OCS Web — Cloud Portal & Church Management Platform

**OCS (Organised Church Service)** is an all-in-one worship presentation, sermon archiving, and cloud management ecosystem built to empower modern ministries, churches, and media teams worldwide.

The **OCS Web Portal** serves as the central cloud command center for church administrators, pastors, and audio-visual teams to manage their church subscriptions, download desktop and mobile companions, monitor church licenses, access technical support, and collaborate seamlessly.

## 🚀 Key Platform Capabilities

### 1. Centralized Church License & Account Hub
- **60-Day Free Trial**: Complete access to the full suite of OCS worship tools with zero upfront commitments.
- **Subscription Management**: Transparent, flexible tiered plans (Mini, Standard, Large, and Premium) tailored for church plants, growing assemblies, and multi-campus auditoriums.
- **Device & Workstation Tracking**: Real-time visibility into active desktop presentation stations and connected mobile companion users under your church license.

### 2. High-Performance Application Distribution
- **One-Click Downloads**: Direct, secure access to the latest release builds of OCS Desktop for **macOS** (Apple Silicon & Intel) and **Windows** (x64), alongside companion links for Android and iOS.
- **Version Audit & Release Notes**: Stay up to date with new features, hymn libraries, and AI enhancements.

### 3. Real-Time Admin Intelligence & Notifications
- **Live WebSocket Event Feed**: Instant notification alerts for support tickets, platform announcements, and community feedback.
- **Analytics & Usage Insights**: Overview of ministry downloads, active church installations, and service archives.

### 4. Ministry Support & Community Engagement
- **Dedicated Support Ticketing**: Direct line to OCS engineering and support for fast assistance before and during Sunday services.
- **Community Suggestions & Testimonials**: Collaborative platform where church tech directors share feature requests and ministry impact stories.

## 🌐 The OCS Ecosystem

| Application | Purpose |
