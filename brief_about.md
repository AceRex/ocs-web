OCS: Making Church Production More Accessible

Live church production shouldn’t feel like something only churches with big budgets can afford.

For many local churches, building a proper media and AV setup is expensive. You need computers, screens, cameras, audio equipment, presentation software, networking, automation tools—and, just as importantly, people with the expertise to set everything up and operate it.
For a growing or smaller church, that can be a serious barrier.

That’s one of the reasons we’re building OCS — Organised Church Software.
OCS is being built specifically with local churches in mind: churches that want better production, better organisation, and more automation without having to build a complex and expensive media infrastructure.

The goal is simple:
Give churches powerful production tools without making the setup unnecessarily complicated.
We’re starting with the essentials and building from there.
🚀 What OCS is bringing together:
🎙️ Live AI Speech & Scripture Tracking
OCS can listen to what is being said and intelligently detect Scripture references, helping operators surface the right passage in real time.
⏱️ Timers & Production Automation
Automate countdowns, service segments, presentations, and other parts of the production workflow so operators can focus on what actually matters.
📱 Mobile Companion
Allow pastors, worship leaders, and stage teams to interact with and control parts of the production workflow directly from their phones.
📂 Session Archives
Turn completed services into organised archives, including audio and formatted transcripts that can be useful for podcasts, sermons, documentation, and recaps.
🖥️ Broadcast & Display Integration
And this isn’t only for smaller churches.
As OCS grows, it can work alongside existing production ecosystems through technologies like OBS, NDI, browser sources, and external displays—making it possible for larger churches to integrate OCS into the systems they already use.
That’s an important part of our vision.
A small church should be able to start simple.
A growing church should be able to scale.
And a large church should be able to integrate.
This is only the beginning.
Our first public increment of OCS is coming in the next few weeks, and we’re getting closer to putting it into the hands of churches and production teams.
We’re building OCS because we believe technology should reduce friction in ministry—not create more of it.
The mission is bigger than building another presentation software.
We want to build a modern operating system for church production.
